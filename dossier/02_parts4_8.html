Job lifecycle Verified

Figure 4.2. Five live states and one terminal exception. Two proposed states give the customer the two moments they most want to see: "your laundry is being washed" and "it is back at your door".

Ops workflow today, no server Verified

  1. Customer books in the wizard; a document is written to bookings_inbox.
  2. Ops opens #/ops, assigns an operator from operators. "Email customer" opens a pre-filled Gmail draft; "Add to Calendar" opens a pre-filled Google Calendar event.
  3. Operator collects; ops marks collected.
  4. Ops opens #/opsinv, enters weight and ironed items; the invoice calculates with the minimum and any manual adjustment; a pre-filled invoice email opens; ops confirms and later marks paid.

Payment today is whatever config/payment says: PayID or BSB text and a Stripe payment link pasted into the email. That is workable for the first jobs and is not a checkout. KEA-13 replaces it.

Colour ratio warning, restated

Navy + red + paper reads "sporting club" the moment red exceeds about 5% of the visible surface or sits on navy. Paper carries the page. Navy gives structure. Red is a signal. If a screen has more than one red element above the fold besides the crown, one of them is wrong.
