Voice and tone New in v2

Say

Short declaratives. Price before adjective. "Booked. Collected Tuesday 9 to 12. Back Wednesday." Use "your operator", never "our driver". Use "we" for the brand promise and "your operator" for the person at the door.

Do not say

"Premium", "hassle-free", "best in Brisbane", any income claim for operators, any turnaround we cannot guarantee, any comparison without its basis. No exclamation marks. No emoji.

Compliance voice

State the limit plainly and once: "We are not a registered NDIS provider." "We do not hold AS/NZS 4146 accreditation, so we do not take clinical linen." The site already does this well; keep it.

Legends Club savings claim: add the basis New in v2

Two ad-hoc loads a week cost $64.00 + $13.50 = $77.50. At the current Solo price of $63.70 the saving is $13.80 a week, 17.8%; at one load the ad-hoc price is $45.50, so Solo costs $18.20 more. Under the Australian Consumer Law an unqualified comparison is a representation the customer can rely on, so the copy carries its basis: "save around $14 a week compared with two ad-hoc loads". The figure is computed from config/pricing at render time and rounded to whole dollars, so a future re-price cannot leave a stale number in the sentence. Done 19 Sep 2026 (Appendix E).
