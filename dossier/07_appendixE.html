Appendix E

Build record: Phases A, B and C

KEA-18 was executed on 19 September 2026 against the copy of record. This appendix records what changed, what it was verified against, and the one defect found during the build that matters more than every design defect in Part 7 combined.

The defect that was not in the audit Found during the build

The customer app and the ops layer never initialised in v4.2. appBoot() called wireWizard() and wireSettings(). Neither function existed anywhere in the 3,586-line file. appBoot threw a ReferenceError on its second statement, so every line after it never ran: the version labels, renderHome, renderAccount, renderForms, renderWizard, renderBookings, renderMessages, appRoute, the not-signed-in notice, the live config/booking and config/contact listeners, and the editor preload of operations.

Every control those two functions were meant to wire was therefore dead: the wizard's quantity steppers, Continue, Previous and Confirm, the recurring switch, the terms gate, and the settings save buttons pdGo, pfGo, adGo, udGo and cmGo, plus the profile switcher sheet. A customer could not complete a booking and could not save a profile, an address or a preference.

This corrects a claim carried in both editions of this dossier. Part 1 of v1 and v2 said "the site works end to end (booking → ops → invoice)". It did not. The functional claim was never tested; the audit that produced Part 7 was a design audit, and a design audit does not press the buttons.

Same-day follow-ups after the build (pre-launch audit, 19 September 2026)

ItemChange
Brand colour alignmentThe site's tokens were derived from a brand guess (red #CC2228, navy #1A3480). Sampling the logo artwork gives red #D84330 and navy #0C265F. The navy token now is the logo navy. The logo red measures 4.41:1 on white, which fails AA for small text, so it ships as --red-brand for the logo, the hero slab and non-text marks, and --red is the same hue deepened to #C43828 (5.33:1 on white, 4.81:1 on paper) for buttons and small text. Numbers that were typed, now computedThe Legends Club comparison on the home page, the pricing-page minimum in the lede, and the hero estimate all read from PX, so a config/pricing override cannot leave stale figures in copy. The saving rounds to whole dollars because the sentence says "around".
Large-linen estimateThe estimator and the ops invoice price linen at the sheet-set rate because the wizard has one quantity control; both now say so and show the three per-item rates, and the invoice points the operator at the adjustment field for blankets and rugs.

Layout review, both viewports (19 September 2026)

All 18 routes screenshotted at 1280 px and 390 px after the brand and content changes. No jumbled or overlapping layouts on either viewport. Three items carried forward rather than fixed, because each is a decision, not a defect:

  • Bulky Items beside Blankets, Rugs & Sheets on the Services and Pricing pages: two adjacent large-textile categories a customer may not distinguish. Options: merge into one table (recommended), or add a one-line distinction to each heading.
  • Desktop chrome in the customer app. At 1280 px the marketing top nav and the phone-style bottom tab bar are both on screen, and the app column is phone-width. Works correctly (the tab bar is fixed to the viewport; a full-page capture misplaces it, a real scroll does not) but duplicates navigation. Options: hide the marketing links inside app routes on desktop, or widen the app column. Owner Callum.
  • Callout styles. The operators page introduces a dashed-border box for "what we cannot tell you yet", a third callout style beside the warning box and the plain card. Deliberate or not, worth a decision.

The accessibility sweep found three defects on its first run, two of them introduced by this build: muted text fell to 4.29:1 when the page surface changed from white to paper underneath it, and the hero eyebrow rule was scoped to the whole home page rather than the hero, so eyebrows on paper bands measured 1.36:1. What has deliberately not changed

  • Pricing. Load $32, delivery $13.50 and minimum $73.50 are unchanged and still read from config/pricing. Two content decisions of 19 September 2026 sit on top of this build: ironing was withdrawn as a service (no wash-fold-iron, no iron-only) and replaced by a per-item Blankets, Rugs & Sheets service at $24 / $32 / $42; and Legends Club rose 30% to $63.70 / $89.70 / $115.70 a week. The home-page saving now computes from those live figures rather than a typed number.
  • Compliance text. Every sentinel is still present and is checked on every build: the NDIS statement, the AS/NZS 4146 statement, the contractor and ABN statements, GST-inclusive pricing and the Australian Consumer Law statement.
  • Footer entity text. Still the placeholder. D13 is Max's to supply and was never going to be invented here.
  • The live artefact. Not republished. The work is committed to the branch and waits on a principal's decision to publish.
What this changes about the plan
  1. M1 was further away than the board showed. "First job operational" assumed a working booking flow. Until this build there was not one. M1 is now genuinely reachable, and the gating items are the ones in Part 16: the entity and ABN, the seeded config, and the first operator.
  2. A design audit is not a functional test. Part 7 was thorough and correct about what it looked at. It looked at rendering. The three scripts above close that gap and now run before every publish.
  3. Add KEA-24: "Ops portal walkthrough against a real booking" — this build verified the customer side end to end but the ops side (assign, collect, weigh, invoice, mark paid) has been exercised only by reading the code. Owner Callum, before the first paid job.
