WHS including chemicals New in v2

An operator's home laundry is a workplace while work is done there, and LaundryKings, as the business that engages them and directs the work, holds a primary duty of care alongside the operator under the Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (Qld). Detergents, stain removers and sanitisers are hazardous chemicals under the WHS Regulation 2011 (Qld) where the Safety Data Sheet says so.

  • Chemical register: a one-page list of approved products with the SDS for each, in OPS03; operators use only listed products. Fragrance-free and eco options are specified here, not chosen ad hoc.
  • Storage and handling: original containers, closed, off the floor, away from children; gloves for stain treatment; never mix chlorine bleach with ammonia-based products; eye-wash access.
  • Fire: dryer lint cleared every load; no overnight unattended dryer runs; smoke alarm in or near the laundry.
  • Manual handling and driving: bag weight limit, trolley for multi-bag collections, no collections after 7 pm, fatigue rule for operators running more than one round.
  • Incident reporting: in-app "report an incident" on the operator view (M3); until then, a form in OPS03 and a phone call to Callum the same day. Notifiable incidents go to Workplace Health and Safety Queensland.
