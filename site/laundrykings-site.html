You operate as an independent business with your own ABN. You use your own equipment and premises, choose your own hours, and decide how much work you take on. We route customers to you, handle the booking platform, brand and customer support, and pay you on an agreed revenue share.

What you need

About Linen Legends

An Australian-owned laundry collection and delivery business, operating Australia-wide.

What we are

Linen Legends collects your laundry, washes it properly, and brings it back. That is the whole proposition. We are not a laundromat you drive to, and we are not an app that disappears when something goes wrong.

We operate through a network of local operators — independent small businesses running collections in their own suburbs — supported by a central booking, routing and customer service platform. That means the person handling your washing lives near you, and the systems behind them are run properly.

Why we built it this way

Two things are broken in this category. Traditional laundries keep hours that working people cannot use, and publish no pricing at all. App-based services solved the convenience problem but treated the people doing the work as disposable, and most of them are gone.

We have built for the middle: published pricing, proper scheduling, and operators who are treated as business partners rather than units of throughput. A round only opens when there is someone good enough to run it.

How we are different

  • Published pricing. Every rate is on this website. You never have to ring to find out what something costs.
  • Price confirmed before work starts. Your operator weighs and counts, then confirms the figure. Nothing proceeds until you have it.
  • Your wash is your wash. Processed on its own cycle, never combined with another household's items.
  • We say what we cannot do. If we are not accredited for something, or not registered for something, we say so on the relevant page rather than letting you find out later.

Coverage

We operate Australia-wide and open household collection windows suburb by suburb, as operators come on. Our coverage page shows where we are live and where we are opening next.

Company details

Trading as Linen Legends. Company and ABN details are published in the footer of every page and on our contact page.

Home / Service Guarantee

Our service guarantee

What we promise, what we will do if we get it wrong, and the limits of both — in plain language.

If the wash is not right

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we will re-wash the affected items at no charge, collected and returned at our cost. If a re-wash will not fix it, we will refund the charge for those items.

If we are late

If we miss your agreed return date and have not contacted you in advance to reschedule, the delivery fee for that order is refunded automatically. You do not need to ask.

If something is damaged or missing

Report it within 7 days of delivery. We will investigate using the item count and weight recorded at collection, and respond within 5 business days with an outcome.

Where we are at fault, we will settle the claim. Settlement is based on the item's current value taking reasonable account of age and condition, not its original purchase price. This is standard practice across the industry and we would rather state it here than argue about it later.

What is not covered

  • Items where the manufacturer's care label was absent, illegible, or where following it caused the damage
  • Pre-existing damage, wear or weakness not visible at collection
  • Colour bleed from items not identified as at-risk at collection
  • Items left in pockets — we check, but we cannot guarantee we catch everything
  • Loss or damage to items we specifically advised against processing and you asked us to proceed with anyway

High-value items

Tell us before collection if any item is worth more than $250. We will either handle it under a specific arrangement or tell you we are not the right service for it. Items above this value that were not declared at collection are covered only to the standard limit.

Insurance

Our operators are required to hold current public liability cover, and the specific policy type and minimum sum insured are set out in the operator agreement. We will confirm the exact cover in place on request — email us and we will send you the detail rather than a vague assurance.

Your rights under Australian Consumer Law

This guarantee sits on top of your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing on this page limits or replaces those rights. Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the ACL, including that services are provided with due care and skill. If you believe we have failed to meet those guarantees, you have remedies available to you regardless of what this page says.

How to make a claim

Contact us through the contact page with your order reference, the affected items, and photographs where relevant. We will acknowledge within one business day.

Privacy policy

How we collect, use, store and disclose your personal information.

Draft for legal review — not yet in force. Date of effect to be inserted on approval.

This policy explains how Linen Legends handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. What we collect

  • Identity and contact details — name, email, phone number, residential or business address.
  • Service information — collection addresses, access instructions, laundry preferences, order history, item counts and weights.
  • Payment information — processed by our third-party payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.
  • Communications — enquiries, messages and records of our correspondence with you.
  • Technical information — IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected when you use this website.
  • Operator applicants — additionally, ABN, equipment details, availability, and the results of identity and background screening.

2. Why we collect it

  • To provide the service you have booked and to contact you about it
  • To process payments and issue invoices
  • To route your job to an operator in your area
  • To handle enquiries, complaints and claims
  • To meet our legal and tax record-keeping obligations
  • To improve our service and plan where we open next

3. Who we share it with

We disclose personal information only where necessary, to:

  • The operator assigned to your job — your name, collection address, access instructions and service preferences. Operators are bound by confidentiality obligations in their agreement with us.
  • Specialist partner cleaners — where your order includes dry cleaning.
  • Service providers — payment processing, SMS and email delivery, cloud hosting and customer support tooling.
  • Plan managers or support coordinators — only where you have engaged us through them, and only invoicing and service information.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where we are legally required to do so.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose it for third-party marketing.

4. Overseas disclosure

Some of our technology providers store data outside Australia. Where that is the case we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles the information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. The specific providers and their locations will be listed here before this policy takes effect.

5. Security

We hold personal information in access-controlled systems and restrict access to those who need it to do their job. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

6. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law — including tax and business record-keeping obligations, generally five years. Unsuccessful operator applications are destroyed within 12 months unless you ask us to keep them on file.

7. Accessing and correcting your information

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. Contact us through the contact page. We will respond within 30 days. If we refuse access we will tell you why in writing.

8. Marketing

We may send you service updates and offers by email or SMS. Every marketing message includes an unsubscribe method. Opting out of marketing does not stop operational messages about a booking you have made.

9. Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality and analytics. You can block cookies in your browser, though some parts of the site may not work correctly.

10. Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first and we will investigate. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published here with its date of effect.
