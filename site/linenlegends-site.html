Services

Everything that goes in a machine, handled.

Household washing through to commercial linen. Tell us what it is at booking and we will tell you what it costs before we touch it.

Wash & Fold

The weekly basket. Washed, dried and folded. Lights and darks are washed separately and count as two loads. One load is about 5 kg.

Blankets, Rugs & Sheets

Large linen that will not fit a domestic machine. Charged per item rather than by weight, so a doona does not swallow your whole order.

Premium Clean

A gentler process for items you would otherwise hand wash. Charged per load you select, not per item. We do not offer ironing.

Dry Cleaning

Suits, dresses, jackets and tailoring, priced per item and returned on hangers with your regular collection.

Bulky Items

Curtains, pet bedding, sleeping bags, work gear. Priced per item after we see it, never guessed at over the phone.

Commercial & Linen

Short-stay changeovers, salons, gyms, cafés and trades. Bulk rates with monthly account billing and collections scheduled around your turnovers.

Note on regulated linen. We do not currently hold accreditation to process clinical or healthcare linen (AS/NZS 4146). If your business requires accredited healthcare laundry, tell us at enquiry and we will say so plainly rather than quote for work we cannot legally do.

How it works

Three steps. Nothing to drop off.

1

You put it out

Book a collection window and leave your bag at the door. No need to be home.

2

Weighed at your door

Your operator weighs and counts on the spot and confirms the final price before starting. You pay after weighing, not before.

3

Washed on its own cycle

Your load is never mixed with another household's. Your detergent, temperature and folding preferences are saved and applied every time.

4

Back on your doormat

Standard return is next day. Folded, sorted and bagged, back where it started.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be home for collection?

No. Leave your bag at the door inside your collection window. Your operator weighs it there, confirms the price with you, and goes.

How is my washing kept separate?

Each household's load runs on its own cycle. Your washing is not combined with anyone else's at any point.

When do I pay?

After weighing. Your operator weighs and counts at collection and confirms the final price before any work begins.

Do you clean healthcare or clinical linen?

Not currently. We do not hold AS/NZS 4146 accreditation, so we will not quote for work that legally requires it. If you need accredited healthcare laundry we will tell you so at enquiry.

Do you work with NDIS participants?

We can be engaged directly by self-managed and plan-managed participants, or by a support coordinator acting on a participant's behalf. We are not currently a registered NDIS provider, so we cannot invoice the NDIA directly for agency-managed plans.

What if something is damaged or missing?

Tell us and we will investigate and respond within five business days. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law are not limited by anything on this page.

Are your prices GST inclusive?

Yes. Every price shown includes GST and is in Australian dollars.

Ready to get your weekend back?

Tell us your suburb and what needs washing. We will confirm your collection window and what it will cost before anything is picked up.
