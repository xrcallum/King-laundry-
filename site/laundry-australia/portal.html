What this tool does today, and what it doesn't

Does: shows an operator's round for the day, calculates the price for a stop from the venture's own published rates, and tracks a job through collection, washing, ready and delivered.

Doesn't yet: receive real bookings (there is no backend feed), identify or screen who is signing in, store anything centrally or share it between devices, or take a payment. Building those is a separate decision — a shared database and a real sign-in are both available, but adding them means deciding what operator and customer data gets stored and for how long, which touches the Privacy Act once real personal information is involved.

How operators are engaged and paid — contractor agreement, ABN, insurance, Super obligations, WHS — is a legal and commercial matter for the venture's advisers, not something this screen decides or implies.
