Our Process

Order

Book online or ring 1800 PICKUP and the Laundrylegends crew will be on their way. Consider it handled.

Pick up

A Laundrylegends van heads your way, one of our team jumps out, collects your washing and we’re gone — quick as you like.

Wash & Dry

Whites sorted from colours, delicates kept separate — then washed, dried and neatly folded. Anything needing the full dry cleaning treatment gets that too, just say the word.

Drop off

Clean, fresh and ready to go — we bring it straight back to your door. All you have to do is put it away.
