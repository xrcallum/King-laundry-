Questions

Before you switch

Do I have to change my email address?
No. Velo connects to the Gmail or Outlook account you already use. Your address, your history and your existing filters stay exactly where they are.
What happens to my mail if I stop paying?
Nothing. Your mail lives with your provider, not with us. Cancelling Velo removes the client, not a single message.
Is my mail used to train models?
No. Assisted replies are conditioned on your own sent mail within your own account, and that data is never pooled across accounts or used for general model training.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Recent mail is cached locally, search runs on-device, and actions you take offline queue and sync the moment you reconnect.
How long does it take to get fast?
Most people are faster than their old client inside a week. Velo ships with a two-minute onboarding that teaches the eight shortcuts that do ninety per cent of the work.

Get your mornings back

Fourteen days, the whole product, no card. If it hasn't changed how you work by day three, walk away.
