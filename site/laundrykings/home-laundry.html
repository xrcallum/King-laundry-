How it works

From your doormat, to washed on its own cycle, back to your doormat.

Step 1

You put it out

Leave your bag at the agreed spot in your booked window. You do not need to be home.

Step 2

Weighed at your door

Step 3

Into your operator's round

Washed on its own cycle

Your load is never mixed with another household. Lights and darks are washed separately.

Back on your doormat

Folded, tagged and returned where the bag was left. Standard next-day return.
