Read the full guarantee +

If the wash is not right

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we re-wash the affected items at no charge, collected and returned at our cost. If a re-wash won’t fix it, we refund the charge for those items.

If we are late

If we miss your agreed return date and haven’t contacted you first to reschedule, the delivery fee for that order is refunded automatically.

If something is damaged or missing

Report it within 7 days of delivery. We investigate using the item count and weight recorded at collection and respond within 5 business days. Settlement is based on the item’s current value, taking reasonable account of age and condition.

Not covered

  • Items with a missing or illegible care label, or where following the label caused the damage
  • Pre-existing damage or wear not visible at collection
  • Colour bleed from items not flagged as at-risk at collection
  • Items left in pockets

This guarantee sits on top of your rights under the Australian Consumer Law and does not limit them. [Terms TBC before launch.]
